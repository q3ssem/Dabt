# Watercolor 4.2 art direction and prompt set

Built-in image generation was used. For 4.2 the 18 PNG sources were converted with ImageMagick to WebP quality 80; 10 two-by-two packs were physically split into 40 individual images. PNG sources are not included in watercolor/art. Total delivered art: 48 WebP files, 3,433,232 bytes. Original source composition descriptions below are retained as design history.
Shared prompt: original refined hand-painted watercolor/gouache, fine restrained ink, airy natural materials, calm central playing area, ornament at outer edges, no labels/numerals/UI/torn parchment. The style does not imply human authorship.

Single backgrounds:
- piano.webp — warm musician's atelier, open ivory/walnut desk, piano edge and brass lamp at corners, no painted keyboard.
- tracking.webp — overhead pale turquoise pond/garden with botanical edge, no painted balls or central targets.
- river.webp — orthographic turquoise river, sandy left24%, water52%, grassy right24%, sky top18%; no boat/animals, those remain interactive.
- pipes.webp — garden waterworks atelier, pale ceramic and brass details at the edge, empty center.
- tower.webp — architect's castle-window cedar workshop, calm center for actual pegs and balls.
- racer.webp — orthographic sage terrain, no road/car; the native moving road is added.
- workbench.webp — quiet explorer's cream desk and botanical/brass edge.

2×2 exact atlases, cell order TL/TR/BL/BR:
- pack-memory — dual observatory / airborne basket ropes / curiosity cabinet / portrait studio.
- pack-home — courtyard memory palace / flashcard desk / personal palace desk / clockmaker.
- pack-spatial — inverted coastline / compass instruments / desert trail / archive.
- pack-logic — origami / mosaic reasoning / pattern crafts / crossword library.
- pack-attention — hawk mountain sky / market street / mirror atelier / glance meadow.
- pack-speed — transport / stepping stones / radar chartroom / surveyor.
- pack-executive — apothecary sorting / sign painter / reverse ribbon / detective desk.
- pack-puzzle-worlds — sea / palace / forest / ice.
- pack-puzzle-craft — locksmith / sculptor / measuring atelier / crystal light.

- pack-scenes — truthful market / garden / beach / airport, no added people or sky-target objects.

icons.webp: exact4×2: lock, stone guardian, balance, pipes / river, parking, logic grid, three-peg Tower of London.
An initial transparent icon attempt with colored fringe was discarded. The delivered revision preserves the eight designs and their positions with a clean dark teal background, no text and no individual border.

Rendering rule: preserve natural object proportions. Crop backgrounds proportionally; do not stretch the atlas to a rectangle. Keep actual problem symbols, directions, colors and motion geometry controlled by the original game.

Runtime pack filenames are pack-NAME-0.webp through pack-NAME-3.webp in TL/TR/BL/BR order; no browser canvas crop. Icons remain one 4-by-2 SVG-viewBox atlas selected by puzzle ID.
