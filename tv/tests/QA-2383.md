# TV 2.3.8.3 pairing candidate

Reported first connection can fail with reconnect message. Prior implementation displayed online after receipt of signed TV state, without evidence TV had registered the phone; initial hello could be lost or race later requests. Exact physical failure is unconfirmed. New handshake waits for signed hello ACK of current TV instance; retries without resetting sockets. Optional manual approval (default automatic) with pending device list on TV, accept/reject and fingerprint, no loss of existing keys or controller permissions, no question changes. New device commands require key binding; known devices can restore presence after transient loss. Pending queue bounded and expires; rejected requests have short cooldown. Nine regression suites including loopback tests for first hello and ACK loss, approval/rejection, conflicting keys and unauthorized commands. No publication before approval.
