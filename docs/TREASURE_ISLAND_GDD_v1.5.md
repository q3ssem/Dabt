# Treasure Island — GDD v1.5

## Narrative premise

**Atlas of Ash: The Island That Erases Roads.** A shifting island removes paths from maps. The crew follows six seals left by cartographer Nader al-Siraj. The apparent treasure is not gold: it is the recovered names and memories of people erased from the island's history.

## Chapter arc

1. The Forgetting Shore — sound pattern, numerical stair.
2. Forest of Lost Names — relational ordering, truth constraint.
3. Lighthouse Without Light — balance, reflection sequence.
4. Underwater Archive — ordering constraints, word-length ordering.
5. Observatory of Ash — dependency path, cyclic movement.
6. Heart of the Island — seal sequence, ethical narrative choice.

## Puzzle contract

Every puzzle must contain: narrative setup, environmental observation, explicit player objective, constraints, at least one meaningful hint, deterministic answer logic, explanation, consequence, and a test case. A puzzle may not rely on hidden trivia or ambiguous wording. When a wrong answer is chosen, explain the reasoning path without revealing the solution too early.

## Future gameplay systems

- Walkable island map with scene transitions.
- Inventory and reusable tools (shell lens, bark lens, lighthouse prism, archive key, star chart).
- Evidence notebook and map annotations.
- Environmental interactions: rotate mirrors, balance weights, route light, arrange artifacts.
- Optional side clues that enrich character backstory but do not block completion.
- Phaser 4 + TypeScript implementation after a vertical slice is approved.

## Current v3.5 implementation

Six chapter cards, twelve narrative multiple-choice puzzles, hints, explanations, rewards/consequences, and local solved-puzzle progress. It is a playable narrative prototype, not yet a top-down exploration game.
