# Planet Journey — GDD v1.5

## Premise

**Spectres of Orbit: The Nine-Station Mission.** Nine automated stations send fragments of an old navigation key. The crew visits each world and reconstructs the key through observation and science-inspired puzzles.

## Planet identity and mechanic

- Moon: shadows and illumination direction.
- Mercury: thermal exposure and timing.
- Venus: atmospheric pressure and safe altitude.
- Earth: water-cycle ordering.
- Mars: dust-storm resource planning.
- Jupiter: storm motion and rotational patterns.
- Saturn: ring-particle density and safe route selection.
- Uranus: axial tilt and orientation.
- Neptune: interpreting sequential wind observations.

## Design rule

The planetary property must change the puzzle logic, not only the background art. Every station needs a science note, a scene that motivates the task, a unique mechanic, a hint, a solution explanation, and a collectible/reward. Avoid presenting simplified gameplay rules as precise scientific simulation.

## Future implementation

- Planet-specific scenes and environmental audio.
- Ship navigation between planets with route planning.
- Data log and science notebook.
- Puzzles using orbit diagrams, shadow projection, resource management, pressure layers, and weather patterns.
- Final synthesis puzzle combining all nine station fragments.

## Current v3.5 implementation

Nine station cards and nine unique content puzzles with local completion tracking. No 3D rendering, orbital simulation, Phaser implementation, or spacecraft control loop is included yet.
