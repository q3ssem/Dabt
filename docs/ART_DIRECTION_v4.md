# Dabt PRO v4 — Art Direction Bible

## Creative target
Two original hand-painted 2D adventures with the warmth, expressive silhouettes, richly layered scenery and animated environmental detail associated with premium illustrated side-scrolling adventures. The reference named by the user is a direction cue only: no Monster Boy characters, level layouts, logos, sprites, UI, or traced assets are used.

## Shared visual grammar
- Side-on 2D composition with readable gameplay silhouettes.
- Inked contour with slight organic irregularity; avoid sterile vector geometry.
- Gouache/watercolor-like color masses, paper grain, dry-brush accents and hand-painted highlights.
- Foreground framing, playable midground, distant parallax silhouettes and atmospheric sky.
- Warm bounce light, soft contact shadows, selective rim light.
- Characters use large readable poses, clear anticipation, squash/stretch kept subtle.
- Props must communicate interaction by silhouette and animation, not color alone.

## Treasure Island palette and materials
Sea-glass teal, lagoon turquoise, sun-bleached sand, coral, leaf greens, oxidized brass, ink-brown wood and parchment. Materials: wet sand, fibrous rope, painted timber, shell nacre, moss, carved basalt, canvas and brass. Mood evolves from inviting coast to uncanny forest, storm lighthouse, flooded archive, ash observatory and volcanic heart.

## Planet Journey visual identities
- Moon: chalk grey, mineral blue, hard sunlight, long black-blue shadows, dusty regolith.
- Mercury: ochre, copper and black shadow; heat shimmer and cratered rock.
- Venus: amber, sulphur gold and dense cloud layers; soft diffused visibility.
- Earth: blue-green, cloud white and living vegetation; water-cycle motifs.
- Mars: iron red, salmon dust and muted violet sky; wind-carved rocks.
- Jupiter: cream, rust and storm rose; banded gas clouds and enormous scale.
- Saturn: pale gold, ice blue and warm umber; rings as sweeping layered ribbons.
- Uranus: icy cyan and muted jade; tilted horizon and unusual seasonal light.
- Neptune: deep ultramarine, cobalt and electric pale blue; fast cloud streaks.

## Animation principles
Idle breathing, two/three-frame blink, anticipation before jump, clear take-off/air/landing poses, foot dust, leaf sway, water shimmer, cloth and rope secondary motion, drifting spores, distant birds, planet dust, ring particles and atmospheric cloud drift. Gameplay objects have a distinct idle loop and a short success animation.

## Technical production targets
- Logical viewport 1100x620; scalable responsive rendering.
- Background layers: 3–5 parallax planes.
- Characters: sprite atlases, 8-direction not required; side-view 4–8 frames per core action as a production target.
- Props: SVG/PNG with transparent background; keep collision shapes separate from painted art.
- UI: parchment/map journal for Island; mission folio/astronomical charts for Planets.
- Accessibility: remappable keyboard, touch controls, reduced motion, readable text and non-color interaction cues.

## Current prototype boundary
v4 prototype uses Canvas 2D procedural hand-drawn-style shapes and layered scenery to validate movement, world identity, interactions, puzzle gates and local persistence. It is a playable art-direction prototype, not a finished sprite-animated commercial art pack. Final production needs bespoke illustrated sprite sheets, tile sets, parallax paintings, sound and animation polish.
